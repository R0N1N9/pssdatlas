# GEO catalogue: transcriptomic / epigenomic datasets on drugs linked to persistent sexual dysfunction

Generated by `analysis/geo_search.py` on 2026-11-18 from NCBI GEO DataSets (E-utilities, `db=gds`, series entries only). Full machine-readable list: `geo_datasets.json`.

**How to read this.** Classification is by keyword rules over the title, summary and sample titles that GEO returns. It is over-inclusive and has not been checked against the papers, except for the entries carrying a *hand-check* note (those were read by eye: GEO summary and, where stated, the sample table). GEO's free-text search can miss a series whose drug is named only in per-sample characteristics, and anything deposited elsewhere (SRA only, ArrayExpress, journal supplements) is out of scope of the search. A dataset being listed here does not mean it shows anything about persistent sexual dysfunction; almost none were designed to.

### Hand-checked gap statement

- SENSORY GANGLIA / SPINAL CORD / GENITAL TISSUE: no GEO series profiles dorsal root ganglia, sensory neurons, spinal cord, pudendal/pelvic nerve, penis, clitoris or genital skin after any of the 17 drugs. The single keyword hit (GSE255683) is an ALS spinal-cord study, not a drug study. The nearest thing is fetal rat anogenital tissue during in-utero finasteride (GSE122494). For a syndrome whose hallmark is genital numbness this is the central gap.
- ADULT WASHOUT DESIGNS: across all drugs, GEO holds one adult treat-then-stop dataset in mammalian brain (GSE67203, fluoxetine, rat hippocampus, 4 samples). Everything else flagged 'persistent' is early-life (perinatal / postnatal / adolescent) exposure read out later.
- ANTIPSYCHOTIC WASHOUT: none in adults. One adolescent-exposure dataset (GSE316091, risperidone, mouse cortex, 2 vs 2). Nothing for olanzapine, quetiapine, aripiprazole, haloperidol or clozapine after discontinuation.
- MIRTAZAPINE: no in-vivo dataset at all and no washout dataset; only a 48 h cortical-neuron culture (GSE63005) and one-replicate screen entries (GSE110256).
- FINASTERIDE / DUTASTERIDE: prostate (mostly cancer or BPH) and toxicogenomic liver/kidney only. No brain, nerve or penile tissue in GEO, no withdrawal arm, and no post-finasteride-syndrome patient dataset: the published patient penile-skin expression study (PMID 34247957, returned by PubMed in this session) was not found in GEO by any query here.
- ISOTRETINOIN: toxicogenomic, teratogenicity (iPSC/embryoid) and skin/sebocyte series only; no neural or genital tissue in vivo, no post-treatment design.
- HUMAN TISSUE FROM AFFECTED PATIENTS (PSSD, PFS, post-retinoid): none in GEO for any tissue.
- THE MOST RELEVANT DATASETS ARE NOT IN GEO: the rat paroxetine treatment-and-withdrawal RNA-seq of hypothalamus and nucleus accumbens (Giatti et al., Mol Neurobiol 2025, PMID 39495228) and the matching finasteride study of hypothalamus and hippocampus (Giatti et al., J Endocrinol Invest 2024, PMID 38493246) were searched for in GEO (db=gds), BioProject and SRA and not found. Their per-sample normalised counts are, however, in the journal supplementary tables; these are what REANALYSIS.md uses. Raw reads appear not to be public (the finasteride paper says data are available on request).
- EPIGENOMIC DATA: methylation / chromatin datasets exist only on-treatment (e.g. fluoxetine H3K27ac GSE193040, haloperidol GSE67755, olanzapine methylation GSE52615, human placental methylation after maternal SSRI GSE203396). None tests whether an epigenetic mark outlasts the drug in an adult.
